# Capsules

Your thoughts, in a pill. A Mac app that keeps little capsules of thought at the edge of your screen: write or speak an idea, a task, a link or a list.

**Download:** [Capsules.dmg](https://github.com/yayamii-1973/capsules/raw/main/Capsules.dmg) · Website: https://yayamii-1973.github.io/capsules

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Heavily inspired by Smartisan Idea Pills. Made by Ivan Kolarov, written by Claude.
