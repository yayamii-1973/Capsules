Capsules

Your thoughts, in a pill. Waiting at the edge of your Mac screen.

Download for Mac Free · Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 14 or later

What it does

Write or speak

Pull the pill from the edge, or press ⌥N. Hold ⌥Space to talk instead.

It knows the kind

Important, task, idea, thought, link or list. One little capsule for each kind sits in the glass.

Lists you tick inside

“List groceries: milk, bread and eggs” becomes a checklist right in the pill.

Calendar, Reminders, Notes

“Call Maria tomorrow at 10am” can go straight to Calendar.

Private by design

Speech is recognized on your Mac. Nothing you say leaves it.

Live translation

Subtitles for what you hear, from the microphone or your Mac’s sound.

How Capsules was born

I’m not a programmer and I’ve never written a line of code. I didn’t write Capsules either. Claude did. I only knew what I wanted.

For years I loved a small marvel called Idea Pills. It was part of Smartisan OS, an Android skin that, for reasons I never quite understood, no longer exists. Idea Pills were little capsules of thought waiting at the edge of the screen. You pulled one out, typed or said something, and the thought was safely put away. I know there is an Android app today that comes close to Smartisan’s, but it is nowhere near as good. When I found myself missing the real thing on my Mac, I asked Claude to bring it back.

I brought the wishes and the whims: “I want the pill to flow out of the screen edge like liquid,” “six little capsules in the glass, like cans in an ad,” “and it should translate live.” Claude wrote every line, patiently, version after version. I tried each one and said “no, not quite yet,” and Claude kept going until it was exactly what I had in mind.

That’s how Capsules became a real Mac app. You write or speak, and it works out by itself whether it’s a task, an idea or a list. Dates go straight to Calendar, and your voice never leaves your computer.

Even this short video, which honestly doesn’t capture how beautiful the app really is in person, was made by Claude. So was the text you’re reading now.

For me this is personal. It’s a piece of something I loved and lost, made again just the way I wanted it. And it showed me that with Claude, anyone can make wonders, even someone who has never written a line of code.

Made out of love for an app and an Android skin that are gone, and written by a friend whose help was anything but small.

Sounds like an ad for Claude? Maybe it is...

— Ivan Kolarov

How to install

  1. Open Capsules.dmg and drag Capsules into Applications.
  2. Open Capsules. The first time, macOS warns that it can’t verify the app, because it isn’t from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Capsules. You only do this once.
  4. Let Capsules download its speech model (about 575 MB). After that, speech works offline.
  5. The pill waits on the right edge of your screen. Everything else is in the menu bar icon, including Settings… ⌘,

Capsules speaks English and Bulgarian and follows your Mac’s language. You can switch it in Settings → General.
